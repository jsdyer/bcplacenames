READING THE RECORD

A name is a clue.
It isn’t the whole story.

Points are reference locations: a creek may be marked at its mouth, a city at its municipal hall. They are not boundaries, trailheads or navigation instructions. A naming decision date is not a founding date.

From the provincial record

Live searches use the BC Geographical Names Office. Official and other recorded names can refer to the same feature. “Rescinded” describes a name’s status; it does not mean the place is abandoned.

Origin stories and pronunciation may be available on the linked official record. Some historical records contain outdated or offensive terminology. Source: Province of British Columbia; source copyright and attribution apply.

Your privacy

Search text and chosen coordinates are sent to the BC service. Map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap for the area you view. No accounts, analytics, photo uploads or saved search history. Shared links include the selected record ID only.
